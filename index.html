Questions

Frequently asked

How much will my tax return cost?

Simple returns start at $85 for federal and Indiana. More forms (kids, a home, self-employment, rentals) add to the price. We quote you up front after looking at your documents, so you'll know the cost before any work begins.

Do I have to come in?

For your first visit, yes. We like to meet you in person, go over your documents together and get your file set up. After that, you can send your paperwork to us securely and electronically, or drop it off at the office.

When should I schedule?

As soon as you have your W-2s and 1099s, usually by early February. February and early April are the busiest weeks, so earlier is better. If you're waiting on a form, we can start and finish when it arrives.

What if I'm behind on filing, or got a letter from the IRS?

It happens more than you'd think. We prepare prior-year and late returns, and we'll help you understand and respond to IRS and Indiana DOR notices. The sooner you bring it in, the more options you usually have.

Why use a local bookkeeper instead of an app?

An app records transactions; a bookkeeper makes sure they're right. Our team reconciles your accounts and catches problems early, and because the same office prepares your return, nothing gets lost between your books and your taxes.

Is there a contract for bookkeeping?

No long-term contract. Plans are month to month. If you're behind, we'll start with a one-time catch-up so your books are current before monthly service begins.

Do you work with QuickBooks?

Yes. Stacy is a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor, and we work in both QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop. We can work in your existing file, clean it up, or set up a new one. If you're not using software yet, we'll recommend what fits your business and budget.

Are you open after tax season?

Yes. Tax season is our busiest time, but bookkeeping, payroll, extensions, amended returns and IRS letters keep us going all year. Off-season help is by appointment.
